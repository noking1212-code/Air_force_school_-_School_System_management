OUR CAMPUS

Airforce School

A Class 12 computer science demo of a school website. The campus name is used for the project only and is not an official Indian Air Force school site.

01 / AIM

Discipline with care

Students learn in an orderly day: assembly, classes, games, and quiet study. Teachers keep families informed through the school portal.

02 / ACADEMICS

Classes I to XII

The demo follows a CBSE-style structure. Results, attendance, and exam dates live in the portal so students and linked parents can follow progress.

03 / HOUSES

Four houses

Falcon, Eagle, Hawk, and Kite. House events appear on the school calendar alongside holidays and examinations.
